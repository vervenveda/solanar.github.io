      Earth systems learning map

      One world. Many connected systems.

      Live measurements belong inside timestamped tools and official scientific sources; these cards remain subject guides.

      ☁️

      Atmosphere

      Air, clouds, pressure, wind, humidity, precipitation, storms, and climate circulation.

      🌊

      Oceans

      Currents, waves, sea-surface temperature, tides, coastal processes, and marine ecosystems.

      💧

      Freshwater

      Rainfall, rivers, lakes, groundwater, floods, drought, watersheds, and water quality.

      ❄️

      Cryosphere

      Snowpack, glaciers, sea ice, permafrost, polar systems, and frozen water storage.

      🦋

      Biosphere

      Forests, wildlife, habitats, migration, pollinators, soils, biodiversity, and seasonal change.

      🌋

      Geosphere

      Earthquakes, volcanoes, plate tectonics, landforms, minerals, erosion, and deep time.

      ☀️

      Solar Activity

      Sunspots, solar wind, flares, coronal mass ejections, aurora, and geomagnetic conditions.

      🛰️

      Near-Earth Space

      Satellites, orbital conditions, debris, radiation, communications, and the space environment.

      Educational boundary: Solanar displays live values only when a source, timestamp, units, and update method can be identified. Official warnings remain official; derived analysis remains labeled as derived.

      Planet timeline

      Read Earth across many scales of time.

      Weather becomes more understandable beside lunar, seasonal, climate, ecological, geological, and planetary cycles.

      NowAlerts, observations, radar, wind, precipitation, daylight, and local conditions
      TodayHourly changes, heat, clouds, storms, Moon, tides, and safety planning
      This WeekForecast patterns, fronts, pressure systems, hazards, and changing air masses
      This MonthLunar cycle, tides, rainfall patterns, phenology, and seasonal transition
      This SeasonMigration, agriculture, drought, tropical systems, snow, heat, and climate rhythms
      This YearTemperature, rainfall, wildfire, oceans, ice, ecosystems, and annual records
      Deep TimeGeology, evolution, plate movement, climate history, and planetary development
