# Teacher / Parent Guide

## Course philosophy
The sequence is spiral rather than repetitive. Learners revisit weather ideas at increasing levels of measurement, systems thinking, mathematical analysis, modeling, safety, and scientific communication.

## Weekly rhythm
**Monday:** Observe & Ask  
**Tuesday:** Measure & Record  
**Wednesday:** Model & Explain  
**Thursday:** Apply & Explore  
**Friday:** Communicate & Master  

Every day includes a lab or investigation and a worksheet.

## Mastery
A weekly quiz contains five questions. Four correct answers = 80% mastery and unlocks the next week.

After all 36 weeks are mastered, the 40-question cumulative final unlocks. A score of 80% or higher displays the completion certificate.

Students may retake weekly quizzes and the final. The application retains the best score.

## Lab safety
Use adult supervision appropriate to age and materials. Outdoor work should stop immediately when weather becomes unsafe. Official emergency alerts and instructions always take priority over coursework.

## Assessment use
The quizzes are designed as mastery checks, not high-stakes one-attempt exams. The final is cumulative and should be completed independently when the teacher/parent considers the student ready.

## Evidence notebook
For best results, maintain a physical or digital weather notebook throughout the year. Repeated entries make the strongest evidence of growth.
