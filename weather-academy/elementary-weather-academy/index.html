Choose by readiness, not only age.

Weather 101–105 are recommended for Grades 1–5 respectively, but enrollment is intentionally open. A Grade 4 learner who is new to weather science can begin with Weather 101 or 102; a younger learner who is ready for data-rich work can move forward with adult guidance. Taking all five creates a spiral elementary meteorology pathway.
