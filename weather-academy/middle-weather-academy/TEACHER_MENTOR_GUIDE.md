# Teacher / Mentor Guide — Weather 201–203

## Daily rhythm

**Monday — Phenomenon & Question**  
Observe before explaining. Define variables and a researchable question.

**Tuesday — Measure & Analyze**  
Collect or analyze quantitative evidence, graph/map it, and identify a defensible pattern.

**Wednesday — Model the System**  
Build or revise a physical, conceptual, diagrammatic, or quantitative model. State assumptions and limitations.

**Thursday — Forecast, Research & Source Check**  
Use Solanar and mapped tools. Distinguish observation, forecast, official alert, model guidance, derived analysis, and background information.

**Friday — CER Briefing & Mastery**  
Use Claim–Evidence–Reasoning, communicate uncertainty, and complete the 10-question mastery quiz.

## Mastery

- Weekly quiz: 10 questions
- Required score: 80% (8/10)
- Learners may retake; best score is retained.
- Week N+1 unlocks only after Week N reaches 80%.
- Final exam unlocks after all 36 weeks are mastered.
- Final exam: 50 questions
- Certificate: 80% final mastery

## Mentor use

Use **Copy Mentor Brief** before or during a NAIB mentorship session. The brief is deliberately concise enough to guide remediation, continuation, or acceleration.

## Safety

Official warnings and emergency instructions always take priority over curriculum activities. A lab or field observation should stop when conditions become unsafe.
