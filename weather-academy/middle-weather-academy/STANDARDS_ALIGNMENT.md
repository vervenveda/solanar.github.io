# Standards Alignment

The Weather 201–203 sequence is designed around major middle-school NGSS weather, climate, hazards, and engineering expectations.

## Core Performance Expectations

- **MS-ESS2-5** — Air-mass motion and complex interactions cause changes in weather; weather prediction is probabilistic.
- **MS-ESS2-6** — Unequal heating and Earth's rotation drive atmospheric and oceanic circulation patterns that influence regional climates.
- **MS-ESS3-2** — Analyze and interpret natural-hazard data to forecast future events and inform mitigation technologies.
- **MS-ESS3-5** — Ask questions that clarify evidence about factors contributing to the rise in global temperatures over the past century.
- **MS-ETS1-1 / MS-ETS1-2** — Define engineering problems using criteria/constraints and evaluate competing solutions.

## Course emphasis

### Weather 201
Heavy emphasis on MS-ESS2-5 and introductory MS-ESS3-2.

### Weather 202
Deepens MS-ESS2-5 through dynamics, radar, severe weather, probability, ensembles, verification, risk communication, and mitigation.

### Weather 203
Heavy emphasis on MS-ESS2-6 and MS-ESS3-5, with MS-ESS3-2 and engineering design integrated into hazard risk and resilience.

The curriculum intentionally uses modeling, data analysis, mathematical reasoning, evidence-based argument, source evaluation, and engineering design throughout rather than treating standards as isolated checklist items.
