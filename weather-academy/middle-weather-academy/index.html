A real middle-school progression.

Weather 201–203 are not repetitions of the elementary sequence. They deepen into atmospheric physics, quantitative weather analysis, radar and satellite interpretation, severe-weather dynamics, probabilistic forecasting, climate systems, model uncertainty, hazard mitigation, and research synthesis.

Weather 201

Atmospheric foundations, air masses, fronts, pressure, radar, satellite, probability, forecast verification, hazards, and mitigation.

Weather 202

Vertical structure, stability, adiabatic processes, jet streams, cyclogenesis, Doppler radar, severe convection, ensembles, and risk communication.

Weather 203

Global circulation, ocean-atmosphere systems, ENSO, climate evidence, models, uncertainty, hazard risk, resilience, and Earth-systems synthesis.
