# Semester II · Weeks 19–36

## Course Equivalents

### ATM 121 — Atmospheric Thermodynamics & Cloud Physics (4 credit-equivalent hours)
Gas laws, hydrostatic balance, moisture variables, saturation, adiabatic processes, stability, cloud formation, microphysics, precipitation and thermodynamic diagrams.

### MAT 152 — Calculus II & Vector Methods for Atmospheric Science (4 credit-equivalent hours)
Integration techniques, differential equations introduction, sequences/series foundations, parametric ideas, vectors, partial-derivative introduction and meteorological applications.

### PHY 152 — University Physics II for Atmospheric Science (4 credit-equivalent hours)
Oscillations, waves, sound, electricity, magnetism, optics, electromagnetic radiation and measurement concepts supporting radar, satellite and atmospheric remote sensing.

### ATM 122 — Observations, Synoptic Analysis & Remote Sensing I (2 credit-equivalent hours)
Station models, METAR-style observations, surface and upper-air maps, radiosondes, radar fundamentals, satellite channels, forecast analysis and verification.

### DAT 121 — Weather Statistics & Scientific Data II (1 credit-equivalent hours)
Descriptive statistics, distributions, probability, error, bias, correlation, regression foundations, uncertainty and ensemble interpretation.

### COM 121 — Forecast Communication & Professional Practice I (1 credit-equivalent hours)
Forecast writing, public-safety communication, broadcast-style explanations, technical climate summaries, ethics and source labeling.

## Weekly Curriculum

### Week 19 — Gas Laws & Atmospheric State
- **Atmospheric Science:** Atmosphere: ideal-gas relationships and equation-of-state reasoning.
- **Mathematics:** Math: integration techniques review and substitution.
- **Physics:** Physics: oscillations and periodic motion.
- **Computing / Data:** Data: calculate state-variable relationships from atmospheric samples.
- **Communication / Professional Practice:** Communication: write assumptions and units explicitly.
- **Laboratory / Applied Work:** Lab: pressure-volume-temperature investigation using safe apparatus or supplied data.

### Week 20 — Hydrostatic Balance
- **Atmospheric Science:** Atmosphere: vertical pressure decrease, gravity and density.
- **Mathematics:** Math: integration applications and differential-equation intuition.
- **Physics:** Physics: waves, frequency, wavelength and phase.
- **Computing / Data:** Data: pressure-versus-altitude numerical analysis.
- **Communication / Professional Practice:** Communication: technical derivation narrative in plain language.
- **Laboratory / Applied Work:** Lab: compare observed/standard pressure profiles.

### Week 21 — Dry Adiabatic Processes
- **Atmospheric Science:** Atmosphere: parcel expansion/compression and dry adiabatic temperature change.
- **Mathematics:** Math: first-order differential-equation concepts.
- **Physics:** Physics: sound and wave propagation.
- **Computing / Data:** Data: plot parcel/environment profiles.
- **Communication / Professional Practice:** Communication: explain adiabatic cooling without saying rising air 'gets farther from the Sun'.
- **Laboratory / Applied Work:** Lab: pressure-change parcel model.

### Week 22 — Moist Adiabatic Processes & Latent Heat
- **Atmospheric Science:** Atmosphere: condensation, latent heating and moist adiabatic behavior.
- **Mathematics:** Math: exponential/logarithmic integration applications.
- **Physics:** Physics: electrostatics and electric fields.
- **Computing / Data:** Data: compare dry/moist parcel traces.
- **Communication / Professional Practice:** Communication: structured thermodynamic explanation.
- **Laboratory / Applied Work:** Lab: model dry versus saturated ascent.

### Week 23 — Atmospheric Stability
- **Atmospheric Science:** Atmosphere: environmental lapse rate, parcel buoyancy, stable/unstable/conditionally unstable layers.
- **Mathematics:** Math: vectors and components.
- **Physics:** Physics: electric potential and energy.
- **Computing / Data:** Data: analyze simplified sounding profiles.
- **Communication / Professional Practice:** Communication: confidence statement tied to vertical-profile evidence.
- **Laboratory / Applied Work:** Lab: classify stability from multiple soundings.

### Week 24 — UNIT III CHECKPOINT — Thermodynamics & Stability
- **Atmospheric Science:** Atmosphere: cumulative Weeks 19–23.
- **Mathematics:** Math: integration/differential-equation/vector checkpoint.
- **Physics:** Physics: waves/electricity checkpoint.
- **Computing / Data:** Data: sounding-analysis practical.
- **Communication / Professional Practice:** Communication: oral thermodynamics defense.
- **Laboratory / Applied Work:** Lab: integrated parcel/stability practical. Unit checkpoint ≥80% required.

### Week 25 — Cloud Microphysics
- **Atmospheric Science:** Atmosphere: aerosol nuclei, droplet growth, collision-coalescence and ice processes.
- **Mathematics:** Math: vector magnitude/direction and introductory multivariable thinking.
- **Physics:** Physics: circuits/current concepts and measurement.
- **Computing / Data:** Data: classify particle-growth scenarios.
- **Communication / Professional Practice:** Communication: scientific-process diagram and caption.
- **Laboratory / Applied Work:** Lab: microphysics model or particle-growth simulation.

### Week 26 — Precipitation Physics
- **Atmospheric Science:** Atmosphere: precipitation growth plus melting/refreezing layers.
- **Mathematics:** Math: parametric representation and rates.
- **Physics:** Physics: magnetism and forces on moving charges.
- **Computing / Data:** Data: diagnose precipitation type from vertical temperature profiles.
- **Communication / Professional Practice:** Communication: communicate uncertainty near transition zones.
- **Laboratory / Applied Work:** Lab: precipitation-type forecast practical.

### Week 27 — SEMESTER MIDTERM II
- **Atmospheric Science:** Atmosphere: cumulative Semester II midterm.
- **Mathematics:** Math: Calculus II/vector methods midterm.
- **Physics:** Physics: waves/electricity/magnetism midterm.
- **Computing / Data:** Data: statistics and sounding practical.
- **Communication / Professional Practice:** Communication: oral scientific briefing.
- **Laboratory / Applied Work:** Lab: integrated midterm case. Every strand ≥80% required.

### Week 28 — Upper-Air Observations & Soundings
- **Atmospheric Science:** Atmosphere: radiosondes, pressure levels, inversions, moisture and wind profiles.
- **Mathematics:** Math: vector applications and introduction to partial rates of change.
- **Physics:** Physics: electromagnetic induction.
- **Computing / Data:** Data: sounding tables, profiles and layer diagnostics.
- **Communication / Professional Practice:** Communication: upper-air diagnostic memo.
- **Laboratory / Applied Work:** Lab: full sounding interpretation.

### Week 29 — Radar Fundamentals
- **Atmospheric Science:** Atmosphere: electromagnetic scattering, reflectivity and sampling limitations.
- **Mathematics:** Math: quantitative scaling and logarithmic-style interpretation where appropriate.
- **Physics:** Physics: electromagnetic waves.
- **Computing / Data:** Data: spatial radar-image interpretation.
- **Communication / Professional Practice:** Communication: distinguish radar observation from warning and forecast.
- **Laboratory / Applied Work:** Lab: track precipitation/storm motion from sequential radar frames.

### Week 30 — Doppler Radar & Severe-Weather Signatures
- **Atmospheric Science:** Atmosphere: radial velocity, convergence, divergence and rotation concepts.
- **Mathematics:** Math: vector projection concept.
- **Physics:** Physics: Doppler/wave applications.
- **Computing / Data:** Data: paired reflectivity/velocity analysis.
- **Communication / Professional Practice:** Communication: cautious hazard-language practice.
- **Laboratory / Applied Work:** Lab: simplified Doppler velocity-signature case.

### Week 31 — Satellite Remote Sensing
- **Atmospheric Science:** Atmosphere: visible, infrared and water-vapor channels; geostationary versus polar perspectives.
- **Mathematics:** Math: functions of multiple variables introduction.
- **Physics:** Physics: optics and radiation interaction.
- **Computing / Data:** Data: compare multi-channel imagery.
- **Communication / Professional Practice:** Communication: write what each channel supports and does not support.
- **Laboratory / Applied Work:** Lab: multispectral cloud/storm interpretation.

### Week 32 — Numerical Weather Prediction Introduction
- **Atmospheric Science:** Atmosphere: model grids, initial conditions, forecast equations concept, parameterization and resolution.
- **Mathematics:** Math: numerical approximation and slope/integration error ideas.
- **Physics:** Physics: computational modeling as approximation of physical laws.
- **Computing / Data:** Data: simple grid/advection experiment.
- **Communication / Professional Practice:** Communication: explain model guidance versus official forecast.
- **Laboratory / Applied Work:** Lab: paper or code-based toy forecast model.

### Week 33 — Probability, Ensembles & Forecast Uncertainty
- **Atmospheric Science:** Atmosphere: deterministic versus probabilistic prediction and ensemble spread.
- **Mathematics:** Math: probability foundations and distributions.
- **Physics:** Physics: measurement uncertainty synthesis.
- **Computing / Data:** Data: ensemble mean, median, range, percentile and threshold probability.
- **Communication / Professional Practice:** Communication: express probability without false certainty.
- **Laboratory / Applied Work:** Lab: ensemble forecast analysis.

### Week 34 — Forecast Verification & Climate Data Foundations
- **Atmospheric Science:** Atmosphere: hits, misses, false alarms, temperature error, bias; weather versus climate datasets.
- **Mathematics:** Math: correlation/regression foundations.
- **Physics:** Physics: experimental validation and model comparison.
- **Computing / Data:** Data: calculate MAE, bias and simple categorical scores; compare daily record to climate normal.
- **Communication / Professional Practice:** Communication: write a verification report that does not cherry-pick.
- **Laboratory / Applied Work:** Lab: verify a forecast set and summarize a climate baseline.

### Week 35 — Three-Career Applied Studio
- **Atmospheric Science:** Operational: produce a short operational forecast package from radar/satellite/model data.
- **Mathematics:** Broadcast: turn the same technical analysis into a 2–3 minute public-facing weather segment.
- **Physics:** Climatology: analyze a multi-year weather/climate dataset and distinguish variability from longer-term pattern.
- **Computing / Data:** Data: all students document reproducible calculations and sources.
- **Communication / Professional Practice:** Communication: audience-specific versions of one evidence base.
- **Laboratory / Applied Work:** Lab: three-station career rotation with mentor evaluation.

### Week 36 — YEAR I COMPREHENSIVE FINAL, ORAL DEFENSE & ADVANCEMENT REVIEW
- **Atmospheric Science:** Atmosphere: cumulative Year I examination spanning observation, thermodynamics, forces, clouds, maps, radar, satellite, models and climate foundations.
- **Mathematics:** Math: cumulative Calculus I–II/vector-method examination.
- **Physics:** Physics: cumulative Physics I–II examination.
- **Computing / Data:** Data: reproducible analysis practical with statistics/probability.
- **Communication / Professional Practice:** Communication: oral defense before mentor/teacher panel.
- **Laboratory / Applied Work:** Lab: integrated meteorological case. Advancement to Year II requires ≥80% on every required final, practical and portfolio competency.
