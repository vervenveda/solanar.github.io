# University & Professional Alignment Notes

## Purpose

The two-year Solanar Professional Meteorology Certificate is intended to approximate the common lower-division academic foundation found in serious meteorology / atmospheric-science study and to prepare a learner for institution-specific placement, challenge, prior-learning or transfer evaluation.

## Common academic foundation intentionally included

- Calculus I and II
- vector methods and early differential-equation concepts
- two semesters of calculus-based university physics
- scientific programming
- weather/climate data analysis
- probability and statistics foundations
- atmospheric composition and structure
- atmospheric thermodynamics
- moisture, clouds and precipitation physics
- atmospheric forces and wind
- observations and instrumentation
- surface and upper-air analysis
- radar
- satellite remote sensing
- numerical weather prediction concepts
- ensemble/probabilistic forecasting
- forecast verification
- weather/climate distinction
- scientific writing and oral communication

## International orientation

The program is deliberately designed around the broad knowledge domains traditionally expected in professional meteorological education: mathematics and physics prerequisites, physical meteorology, dynamic/synoptic reasoning, observing systems, data processing, forecasting, climate science and professional communication.

## Receiving-university review

For a real university evaluation, provide:
- full syllabus
- weekly schedule
- learning outcomes
- laboratory list
- contact/learning hours
- assessment rubrics
- examinations
- student transcript
- graded portfolio
- instructor qualifications
- mastery policy
- course descriptions

A receiving university may still require its own examination, laboratory residency, proctored assessment, accreditation review or additional prerequisite courses.
