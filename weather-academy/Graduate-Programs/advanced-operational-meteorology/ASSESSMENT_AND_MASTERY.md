# Graduate Mastery & Assessment Policy

## Hard 80% rule
No required competency may be averaged away.

Each instructional week requires:
1. Monday graduate competency ≥80%
2. Tuesday quantitative/mathematical competency ≥80%
3. Wednesday case/diagnostic competency ≥80%
4. Thursday data/model/remote-sensing competency ≥80%
5. Friday research/operations competency ≥80%
6. Required professional evidence forms complete
7. Weekly synthesis/case/oral practical ≥80%
8. Every sixth week: graduate qualifying checkpoint ≥80%

Semester II requires Semester I completion.

## Graduate final
Each independently ≥80%:
- cumulative written meteorology qualifying exam;
- quantitative derivation/mathematics/data-analysis exam;
- displaced-real-time forecast and warning operations case;
- research manuscript and reproducibility audit;
- oral defense / professional briefing exam.

## Credential
Passing unlocks a Solanar Graduate Certificate of Completion in Advanced Operational Meteorology, concentration in Mesoscale & Severe Weather Forecasting.

It is not a university degree, accredited academic credit, government license, AMS credential, or guarantee of professional eligibility.
