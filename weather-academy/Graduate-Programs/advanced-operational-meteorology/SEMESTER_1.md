# Graduate Semester I · Weeks 1–18

## Week 1 — Conservation Laws, Scaling & Graduate Diagnostic Method
- **Monday — Graduate Theory & Derivation:** Derive and interpret atmospheric mass, momentum and thermodynamic conservation laws; distinguish Eulerian and Lagrangian viewpoints.
- **Tuesday — Mathematics & Quantitative Methods:** Scale and nondimensionalize representative terms; document assumptions, units and error sensitivity.
- **Wednesday — Case / Diagnostic Laboratory:** Diagnose dominant force balances and time scales from an archived multi-scale case.
- **Thursday — Models, Radar, Satellite & Data:** Compare observations with analyzed/model fields and identify where balance approximations fail.
- **Friday — Research / Forecast Operations:** Establish the graduate evidence notebook, source ledger and reproducibility protocol; defend one approximation orally.
- **Higher Learning mathematics:** Calculus II, Differential Equations
- **Required evidence:** Graduate Case Diagnostic, Reproducible Analysis Record

## Week 2 — Rotating Stratified Flow & Mesoscale Balance

- **Wednesday — Case / Diagnostic Laboratory:** Analyze terrain, boundary-layer and ageostrophic circulations from surface and upper-air observations.
- **Thursday — Models, Radar, Satellite & Data:** Evaluate model cross-sections and fields for balance, blocking and mesoscale circulations.

- **Required evidence:** Graduate Case Diagnostic, Graduate Forecast Shift Log
