# NAIB / Faculty Graduate Mentor Guide

NAIB and human faculty use a non-advancement rule: identify the first unmet prerequisite and remediate it before progression.

Graduate mentoring should challenge:
- physical reasoning and assumptions;
- mathematical defensibility;
- source and valid-time discipline;
- distinction among observation, model guidance, derived analysis and official products;
- competing hypotheses and willingness to update;
- reproducibility;
- verification and hindsight bias;
- ethical communication under uncertainty.

NAIB supports questioning, rehearsal and feedback. Human examiner/instructor judgment remains required for verified practical, qualifying, capstone and oral-defense scores.
