# Mathematics Integration

This concentration is deliberately cross-departmental. Full mathematics instruction remains in Khaemenes Higher Learning; graduate meteorology applies that mathematics to atmospheric problems.

## Core
- Calculus II — integration, series and continuous physical-model applications.
- Linear Algebra — vectors, matrices, eigenstructure, least squares, SVD, state vectors and data assimilation.
- Differential Equations — dynamical systems, stability, numerical methods and atmospheric rate equations.
- Statistics — probability, inference, regression, resampling, ensemble forecasting, calibration and verification.

## Research distinction
- Introduction to Proofs — rigorous reasoning, assumptions and research writing.
- Real & Complex Analysis — recommended advanced extension for mathematically intensive research.
- Topology & Differential Geometry — optional extension for geophysical-fluid-dynamics research.

A meteorology gate does not automatically mark a full mathematics course complete. Course completion remains governed by the Mathematics Department's own assessment architecture.
