# Graduate Semester I — Tropical Dynamics, Genesis & Observation

## Week 1 — Tropical Atmosphere as a Distinct Dynamical Regime
- **Monday — Tropical Theory & Dynamics:** Contrast tropical and extratropical dynamics: weak Coriolis near the equator, deep convection, moist static energy, weak temperature gradients aloft and large-scale overturning.
- **Tuesday — Mathematics & Quantitative Analysis:** Calculate Coriolis parameter/inertial scales and review thermodynamic/energy budgets and nondimensional reasoning.
- **Wednesday — Observation / Case Laboratory:** Map tropical latitude belts, ITCZ/monsoon regions and representative pressure/wind/moisture structures.
- **Thursday — Forecast Guidance & Operations:** Compare tropical analyses with midlatitude analyses and identify which familiar approximations weaken or change.
- **Friday — Research, Hazards & Defense:** Establish graduate source/evidence notebook and defend why tropical forecasting requires a distinct conceptual model.
- **Higher Learning mathematics:** Calculus II, Differential Equations
- **Required evidence:** Graduate Tropical Synoptic Analysis
