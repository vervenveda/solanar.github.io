# Mathematics Integration — Track 03
Core: Calculus II, Differential Equations, Linear Algebra and Statistics. Research extensions: Introduction to Proofs and Real & Complex Analysis.

Applications include vortex dynamics, angular momentum, steering vectors, great-circle track error, ensemble spread, intensity probabilities, forecast verification, coupled atmosphere–ocean reasoning and research inference.

Meteorology mastery does not automatically complete the corresponding Higher Learning mathematics course; each mathematics course retains its own independent mastery architecture.
