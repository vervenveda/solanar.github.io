# Mathematics Integration — Track 02

Track 02 is deliberately cross-departmental.

## Core quantitative spine
- **Linear Algebra:** state vectors, matrices, least squares, eigenstructure, SVD, covariance and inverse problems.
- **Differential Equations:** atmospheric systems, numerical integration, stability, dynamical prediction and error growth.
- **Statistics:** estimation, Bayesian foundations, covariance, ensembles, verification, calibration and ML evaluation.
- **Calculus II:** integration, Taylor expansions/series, continuous equations and numerical approximations.

## Graduate research reasoning
- **Introduction to Proofs:** formal assumptions, convergence reasoning and defensible methods.
- **Real & Complex Analysis:** optional research-distinction extension for rigorous numerical analysis.

Meteorology completion does not automatically complete a Higher Learning mathematics course. Each mathematics course retains its own independent mastery requirements.
