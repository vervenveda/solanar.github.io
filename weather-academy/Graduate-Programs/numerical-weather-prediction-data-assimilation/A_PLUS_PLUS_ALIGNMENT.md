# A++ Graduate Design & External Benchmark Notes

Track 02 intentionally combines academic numerical meteorology, professional operational NWP/DA concepts, rigorous mathematics, transparent computational experiments, reproducibility, verification and defended capstone work.

## Benchmark families used
- ECMWF contemporary data-assimilation training: OI, 3D-Var, 4D-Var, Kalman filtering, ensemble/hybrid methods, background-error modeling, bias correction, conventional/actively sensed observations, radiances, tangent-linear/adjoint methods, coupled DA and ML in DA/NWP workflows.
- WMO development of skills/knowledge for operational meteorologists using NWP and competencies for high-resolution NWP systems.
- NOAA/NCEP operational forecast and assimilation systems, including GDAS/GFS/GEFS, hybrid ensemble-variational DA and model-data access.
- Khaemenes Higher Learning mathematics: Calculus II, Linear Algebra, Differential Equations, Statistics and research-level reasoning.

## What “A++” means here
A++ denotes the internal design target: unusually broad integration, high quantitative expectations, hard mastery, repeated computation, research, reproducibility and oral defense.

It does **not** claim universal accreditation, transfer equivalence, government qualification, or formal superiority to every university or professional program.
