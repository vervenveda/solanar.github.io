# NAIB / Faculty Mentor Guide — Track 02

Graduate mentoring must require explanation, not button-pushing.

Challenge the learner to:
- state the governing assumptions;
- distinguish numerical, model-physics, initial-condition and observation errors;
- explain matrix/probability/ODE reasoning;
- document configuration and provenance;
- inspect innovations, residuals, ensemble spread and verification;
- use matched baselines;
- avoid hindsight and confirmation bias;
- separate correlation from causal claims;
- explain why a method failed as carefully as why it succeeded;
- identify where linear, Gaussian or independence assumptions are only approximations;
- maintain a reproducible record.

NAIB supports questioning and rehearsal. Human examiner/instructor judgment is required for verified graduate practicals, checkpoints, capstone and oral-defense scores.
