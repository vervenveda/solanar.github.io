# ALIGNMENT — Graduate Track 04

Track 04 was constructed as an advanced graduate/professional climate-science concentration.

2026 primary-source benchmark areas incorporated:
- Princeton Atmospheric and Oceanic Sciences: coupled Earth-system dynamics, energy balance, atmosphere/ocean/land/cryosphere coupling, feedbacks, climate-model hierarchy and paleoclimate.
- MIT PAOC: atmosphere/ocean dynamics, statistics, general circulation, large-scale dynamics, observation and stability.
- MIT Atmospheric and Oceanic Modeling: numerical methods, formulation and parameterizations used in atmosphere/ocean models.
- NSF NCAR CESM training: simulating the climate system, running models, modifying components and analyzing model output.
- WCRP CMIP7: DECK, Community MIPs, Assessment Fast Track and next-generation coordinated experiment design.
- CMIP7 Rapid Evaluation Framework: systematic model-evaluation diagnostics and reproducible evaluation workflow.
- NOAA GFDL climate modeling: ensembles, natural variability, forcing, attribution, model disagreement, resolution and Earth-system development.
- IPCC Working Group I: physical climate evidence, observations, attribution, projections and calibrated uncertainty.
