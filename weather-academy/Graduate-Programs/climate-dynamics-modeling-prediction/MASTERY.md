Threshold: 80%.

1. Five daily competencies each >=80%.
2. Weekly evidence record completed and verified.
3. Weekly synthesis/practical/oral >=80%.
4. Qualifying checkpoint >=80% when assigned.
5. Only then does the next week unlock.

Semester sequence:

- semester comprehensive independently >=80%;
- Semester II locked until Semester I completes;
- Final locked until Semester II completes.

Final sequence:
1. Climate dynamics / Earth-system written exam >=80%.
2. Mathematics / statistics / computational reasoning >=80%.
3. Model experiment / evaluation practical >=80%.
4. Prediction / projection / uncertainty case >=80%.
5. Capstone / reproducibility / oral defense >=80%.

No average can repair a failed required gate.
