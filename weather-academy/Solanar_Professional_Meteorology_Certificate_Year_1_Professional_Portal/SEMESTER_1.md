# Semester I · Weeks 1–18

## Course Equivalents

### ATM 111 — Foundations of Atmospheric Science I (4 credit-equivalent hours)
Atmospheric composition and structure; weather observation; temperature; pressure; moisture; clouds; forces; winds; air masses; fronts; cyclone structure; forecast fundamentals.

### MAT 151 — Calculus I for Atmospheric Science (4 credit-equivalent hours)
Functions, limits, derivatives, applications of derivatives, introductory integration, rates of change, optimization, graph interpretation and meteorological applications.

### PHY 151 — University Physics I for Atmospheric Science (4 credit-equivalent hours)
Measurement, vectors, kinematics, Newtonian mechanics, work/energy, momentum, rotation, gravitation, fluids, temperature and introductory thermodynamics.

### CSC 111 — Scientific Programming & Weather Data I (2 credit-equivalent hours)
Python-style scientific reasoning, variables, control flow, functions, tables, CSV data, plotting, data cleaning, reproducible notebooks and basic numerical methods.

### COM 111 — Meteorological Communication I (2 credit-equivalent hours)
Scientific writing, source discipline, weather terminology, briefing structure, audience adaptation, uncertainty language, visual communication and oral presentation.

## Weekly Curriculum

### Week 1 — Scientific Foundations & Measurement
- **Atmospheric Science:** Atmosphere: course orientation, atmosphere as a physical system, SI units, metadata and observation quality.
- **Mathematics:** Math: functions, graphs, units and dimensional reasoning.
- **Physics:** Physics: measurement, vectors and uncertainty.
- **Computing / Data:** Data: notebook setup, variables, tables and plotting.
- **Communication / Professional Practice:** Communication: distinguish observation, interpretation, forecast and warning.
- **Laboratory / Applied Work:** Lab: establish a personal weather station protocol and collect a baseline dataset.

### Week 2 — Atmospheric Composition, Pressure & Density
- **Atmospheric Science:** Atmosphere: composition, vertical structure, pressure and density.
- **Mathematics:** Math: limits and rates of change.
- **Physics:** Physics: vectors and one-dimensional motion.
- **Computing / Data:** Data: import, clean and visualize temperature/pressure records.
- **Communication / Professional Practice:** Communication: write a source-labeled observation summary.
- **Laboratory / Applied Work:** Lab: graph pressure/density with altitude from a supplied standard-atmosphere dataset.

### Week 3 — Temperature, Heat & Energy
- **Atmospheric Science:** Atmosphere: temperature scales, heat, heat capacity and weather relevance.
- **Mathematics:** Math: derivative concept and instantaneous rate of change.
- **Physics:** Physics: kinematics and acceleration.
- **Computing / Data:** Data: calculate daily maxima, minima, range and rate of temperature change.
- **Communication / Professional Practice:** Communication: explain temperature versus heat without oversimplifying.
- **Laboratory / Applied Work:** Lab: compare heating/cooling rates of contrasting surfaces.

### Week 4 — Solar Radiation & Surface Heating
- **Atmospheric Science:** Atmosphere: solar geometry, absorption, reflection and surface-energy concepts.
- **Mathematics:** Math: derivative rules.
- **Physics:** Physics: Newton's laws and force diagrams.
- **Computing / Data:** Data: plot irradiance/temperature relationships.
- **Communication / Professional Practice:** Communication: produce a concise solar-heating explainer.
- **Laboratory / Applied Work:** Lab: lamp/globe or surface-albedo heating investigation.

### Week 5 — Surface Energy Balance
- **Atmospheric Science:** Atmosphere: sensible heat, latent heat, ground heat and radiative balance.
- **Mathematics:** Math: product/quotient/chain rules.
- **Physics:** Physics: work, kinetic energy and potential energy.
- **Computing / Data:** Data: calculate and graph simplified energy-budget terms.
- **Communication / Professional Practice:** Communication: construct an evidence-based diagram with caption.
- **Laboratory / Applied Work:** Lab: simplified daytime surface-energy budget.

### Week 6 — UNIT I CHECKPOINT — Measurement, Energy & Core Mathematics
- **Atmospheric Science:** Atmosphere: cumulative review Weeks 1–5.
- **Mathematics:** Math: cumulative functions/limits/derivatives review.
- **Physics:** Physics: measurement, motion, forces and energy review.
- **Computing / Data:** Data: reproducibility and data-quality audit.
- **Communication / Professional Practice:** Communication: five-minute technical briefing.
- **Laboratory / Applied Work:** Lab: integrated energy-and-observation practical. Unit checkpoint ≥80% required.

### Week 7 — Water Vapor & Humidity
- **Atmospheric Science:** Atmosphere: vapor pressure, relative humidity, dew point and moisture variables.
- **Mathematics:** Math: implicit rates and related rates.
- **Physics:** Physics: momentum and impulse.
- **Computing / Data:** Data: calculate dew-point depression and compare humidity metrics.
- **Communication / Professional Practice:** Communication: explain why relative humidity changes with temperature.
- **Laboratory / Applied Work:** Lab: humidity/evaporation comparison under two environments.

### Week 8 — Saturation, Condensation & Clouds
- **Atmospheric Science:** Atmosphere: saturation, condensation nuclei, cloud-base formation and cloud families.
- **Mathematics:** Math: extrema and optimization.
- **Physics:** Physics: circular motion and rotation introduction.
- **Computing / Data:** Data: cloud observations and categorical frequency analysis.
- **Communication / Professional Practice:** Communication: cloud identification briefing using evidence rather than appearance alone.
- **Laboratory / Applied Work:** Lab: controlled condensation/cloud demonstration and cloud observation log.

### Week 9 — SEMESTER MIDTERM I
- **Atmospheric Science:** Atmosphere: midterm assessment of Weeks 1–8.
- **Mathematics:** Math: calculus midterm.
- **Physics:** Physics: mechanics midterm.
- **Computing / Data:** Data: practical data-cleaning and graphing assessment.
- **Communication / Professional Practice:** Communication: oral defense of one laboratory result.
- **Laboratory / Applied Work:** Lab: integrated midterm practical. Every strand ≥80% required.

### Week 10 — Pressure Gradients & Wind
- **Atmospheric Science:** Atmosphere: pressure-gradient force and wind initiation.
- **Mathematics:** Math: introduction to antiderivatives.
- **Physics:** Physics: gravitation and potential fields.
- **Computing / Data:** Data: contour spacing and gradient comparison.
- **Communication / Professional Practice:** Communication: explain why pressure maps can imply wind without claiming pressure alone determines actual wind.
- **Laboratory / Applied Work:** Lab: pressure-map gradient analysis and wind comparison.

### Week 11 — Coriolis Effect & Large-Scale Flow
- **Atmospheric Science:** Atmosphere: rotating reference frames, Coriolis effect and latitude dependence.
- **Mathematics:** Math: definite integrals and area accumulation.
- **Physics:** Physics: rotational motion and angular momentum.
- **Computing / Data:** Data: vector-direction plotting.
- **Communication / Professional Practice:** Communication: correct common Coriolis misconceptions.
- **Laboratory / Applied Work:** Lab: rotating-platform or rotating-paper trajectory model.

### Week 12 — UNIT II CHECKPOINT — Moisture, Clouds & Atmospheric Forces
- **Atmospheric Science:** Atmosphere: cumulative review Weeks 7–11.
- **Mathematics:** Math: derivative applications plus introductory integration.
- **Physics:** Physics: momentum/rotation/gravitation review.
- **Computing / Data:** Data: combined moisture/wind analysis.
- **Communication / Professional Practice:** Communication: public explainer plus technical appendix.
- **Laboratory / Applied Work:** Lab: integrated force/moisture practical. Unit checkpoint ≥80% required.

### Week 13 — Friction, Boundary Layer & Local Winds
- **Atmospheric Science:** Atmosphere: friction, turbulent mixing, sea/land breezes and terrain circulations.
- **Mathematics:** Math: Fundamental Theorem of Calculus concept.
- **Physics:** Physics: fluids, pressure and buoyancy introduction.
- **Computing / Data:** Data: compare coastal/inland temperature and wind records.
- **Communication / Professional Practice:** Communication: create a local-wind forecast narrative.
- **Laboratory / Applied Work:** Lab: land-water differential-heating circulation model.

### Week 14 — Air Masses & Fronts
- **Atmospheric Science:** Atmosphere: source regions, modification and frontal boundaries.
- **Mathematics:** Math: accumulation and meteorological rate problems.
- **Physics:** Physics: fluid statics and density.
- **Computing / Data:** Data: map temperature/dew-point gradients.
- **Communication / Professional Practice:** Communication: write a frontal-passage briefing with timing uncertainty.
- **Laboratory / Applied Work:** Lab: identify air masses/fronts from a station dataset.

### Week 15 — Cyclones, Anticyclones & Weather Systems
- **Atmospheric Science:** Atmosphere: pressure-system structure, circulation and basic midlatitude cyclone evolution.
- **Mathematics:** Math: applied integration review.
- **Physics:** Physics: fluid dynamics concepts and Bernoulli limitations.
- **Computing / Data:** Data: track pressure-center motion.
- **Communication / Professional Practice:** Communication: one-page storm-system analysis.
- **Laboratory / Applied Work:** Lab: sequence and annotate a developing cyclone.

### Week 16 — Weather Observations & Instrumentation
- **Atmospheric Science:** Atmosphere: thermometer, barometer, hygrometer, anemometer, precipitation gauges and siting.
- **Mathematics:** Math: quantitative error and percent difference.
- **Physics:** Physics: calibration, sensor response and experimental error.
- **Computing / Data:** Data: quality-control rules and outlier review.
- **Communication / Professional Practice:** Communication: instrument-method section written in formal scientific style.
- **Laboratory / Applied Work:** Lab: instrument comparison/calibration practical.

### Week 17 — Station Models, Weather Maps & First Forecast
- **Atmospheric Science:** Atmosphere: station plots, map analysis, fronts and basic short-range forecasting.
- **Mathematics:** Math: cumulative calculus synthesis.
- **Physics:** Physics: mechanics/fluids/thermal synthesis.
- **Computing / Data:** Data: build a small surface-analysis dataset.
- **Communication / Professional Practice:** Communication: first 3-minute operational/broadcast forecast plus written technical forecast.
- **Laboratory / Applied Work:** Lab: analyze a surface map and verify a 12–24 hour forecast.

### Week 18 — SEMESTER I FINAL & PORTFOLIO DEFENSE
- **Atmospheric Science:** Atmosphere: comprehensive Semester I final.
- **Mathematics:** Math: comprehensive Calculus I final.
- **Physics:** Physics: comprehensive Physics I final.
- **Computing / Data:** Data: reproducible weather-data practical.
- **Communication / Professional Practice:** Communication: portfolio presentation and oral examination.
- **Laboratory / Applied Work:** Lab: integrated forecast-and-analysis final practical. Semester completion requires every course strand ≥80%.
