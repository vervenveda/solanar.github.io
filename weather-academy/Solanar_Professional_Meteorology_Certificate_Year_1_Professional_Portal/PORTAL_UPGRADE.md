The Year I curriculum is now implemented as an adult professional-learning system rather than only a syllabus package.

- Semester II gated portal

- Assigned app/tool mapping by lesson/week
- Professional pop-up learning windows with new-tab fallback
- Semester II lock until Semester I mastery
- Year I completion record/certificate after both semesters

## Mastery

Each week requires:
1. Atmospheric Science verified score ≥80%
2. Mathematics verified score ≥80%
3. Physics verified score ≥80%
4. Computing/Data verified score ≥80%
5. Communication verified score ≥80%
6. Laboratory/Applied verified score ≥80%
7. Required professional form(s) completed
8. Weekly 10-question mastery assessment ≥80%

At every sixth week, a cumulative unit checkpoint must also be ≥80%.

Semester completion requires the full semester plus its comprehensive examination/portfolio defense at ≥80%.

The second semester cannot open through the normal program interface until Semester I is complete.
