# Assessment, Mastery & Transcript Framework

## 80% means mastery, not averaging

The program uses non-compensatory mastery. A 95% in communication does not erase a 70% in calculus. Each required domain must reach 80%.

## Required records

Maintain:
- course code and title
- instructional weeks
- contact/learning hours
- assignments
- problem sets
- laboratory reports
- coding/data notebooks
- quizzes
- unit checkpoints
- midterms
- finals
- oral examinations
- portfolio artifacts
- attempt history
- best mastery score
- instructor/mentor verification

## Suggested grading evidence

**Atmospheric science:** written exams, map/sounding analysis, forecast reasoning  
**Mathematics:** worked problems with derivations  
**Physics:** quantitative problems and laboratory practicals  
**Data:** reproducible notebooks and statistical analysis  
**Communication:** technical memo, forecast script, oral briefing, source use  
**Laboratory:** method, data quality, calculations, interpretation, uncertainty and safety

## Advancement

Semester II opens only after Semester I required finals/practicals are ≥80%.

Year II opens only after:
1. all 36 Year I weeks are mastered,
2. all Year I course strands are ≥80%,
3. the Year I comprehensive final is ≥80%,
4. the laboratory/data practical is ≥80%,
5. the oral defense is ≥80%.

NAIB or another mentor should identify the first unmet prerequisite and assign remediation rather than advance the learner.
