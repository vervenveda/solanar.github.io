# Semester IV · Year II Weeks 19–36

## Course equivalents

### ATM 232 — Dynamical Meteorology II (3 credit-equivalent hours)
Waves, instability, potential vorticity, ageostrophic circulations, jet/front dynamics and multiscale interactions.

### ATM 242 — High-Impact Weather (4 credit-equivalent hours)
Deep convection, supercells, tornadoes, hail, damaging wind, winter weather, fire weather and warning decision science.

### ATM 252 — Tropical, Aviation & Marine Meteorology (3 credit-equivalent hours)
Tropical cyclones, aviation hazards, marine boundary layer, fog, waves, coastal weather and specialized services.

### ATM 262 — NWP II, Ensembles & Data Assimilation (3 credit-equivalent hours)
Data assimilation, ensemble design, probabilistic guidance, calibration, post-processing, bias and model diagnostics.

### HYD 242 — Hydrometeorology & Boundary-Layer Applications (2 credit-equivalent hours)
QPF, runoff/flood response, land-atmosphere exchange, terrain, boundary-layer processes and applied hazards.

### OPS 242 — Operational Forecasting Practicum & Capstone (2 credit-equivalent hours)
Multi-hazard forecast shifts, partner briefings, verification, forecast discussions, capstone operations and oral defense.

## Weekly curriculum

### Year II Week 19 · Program Week 55 — Convective Initiation, CAPE/CIN & Storm Environment
- **Monday — Dynamics / Theory:** Diagnose buoyancy, parcel choice, CAPE/CIN, lapse rates, moisture depth, forcing and convective initiation failure modes.
- **Tuesday — Quantitative Methods:** Calculate parcel/environment temperature differences, layer lapse rates and simplified buoyancy/CAPE relationships.
- **Wednesday — Analysis Laboratory:** Analyze soundings, surface boundaries and forcing to assess initiation timing and conditionality.
- **Thursday — Remote Sensing / NWP:** Use radar/satellite/SPC mesoscale products and convection-allowing models to track pre-storm evolution.
- **Friday — Operational Forecast Shift:** Issue a conditional thunderstorm forecast with explicit triggers, failure modes and confidence.
- **Required forms:** Sounding & Hodograph Severe-Weather Analysis, Mesoscale Analysis Worksheet, Professional Forecast Briefing
- **Competencies:** convection, cape-cin, conditional-forecast

### Year II Week 20 · Program Week 56 — Deep Convection, Supercells & Hodographs
- **Monday — Dynamics / Theory:** Study storm modes, vertical shear, hodographs, storm-relative flow, mesocyclones and supercell structure.
- **Tuesday — Quantitative Methods:** Compute shear vectors, storm-relative wind concepts and qualitative helicity relationships.
- **Wednesday — Analysis Laboratory:** Classify storm environment and expected mode using soundings/hodographs and boundary orientation.
- **Thursday — Remote Sensing / NWP:** Track developing convection with radar, satellite and high-resolution model guidance.
- **Friday — Operational Forecast Shift:** Produce a severe-weather briefing separating environmental potential from observed storm behavior.
- **Required forms:** Sounding & Hodograph Severe-Weather Analysis, Dual-Polarization Radar Analysis, Severe Weather Decision Log
- **Competencies:** supercells, shear, storm-mode

### Year II Week 21 · Program Week 57 — Tornado, Hail & Damaging-Wind Decision Science
- **Monday — Dynamics / Theory:** Integrate environmental and radar evidence for tornado, hail and wind hazards; study warning decision uncertainty and false-alarm tradeoffs.
- **Tuesday — Quantitative Methods:** Apply probability, base-rate and conditional-risk reasoning to hazard decisions.
- **Wednesday — Analysis Laboratory:** Analyze an archived severe-weather event and identify signal evolution, reports and decision points.
- **Thursday — Remote Sensing / NWP:** Use radar/mesoanalysis/satellite evidence while documenting artifacts and uncertainty.
- **Friday — Operational Forecast Shift:** Complete a simulated warning/impact decision log and partner briefing; official-warning language is clearly separated from training simulation.
- **Required forms:** Severe Weather Decision Log, Dual-Polarization Radar Analysis, Impact-Based Decision Support Brief
- **Competencies:** severe-weather, decision-science, communication

### Year II Week 22 · Program Week 58 — Winter Weather, Precipitation Type & Bands
- **Monday — Dynamics / Theory:** Study snow/ice microphysics, warm noses, wet-bulb effects, frontogenesis, deformation bands and mesoscale snow processes.
- **Tuesday — Quantitative Methods:** Calculate thickness/wet-bulb approximations, thermal profiles and simple snow/liquid ratios with uncertainty.
- **Wednesday — Analysis Laboratory:** Diagnose precipitation type, banding potential and transition zones from soundings/cross-sections.
- **Thursday — Remote Sensing / NWP:** Compare radar, satellite and ensemble guidance for band placement and precipitation-type uncertainty.
- **Friday — Operational Forecast Shift:** Prepare a winter-weather impact forecast including confidence ranges and bust scenarios.
- **Required forms:** Winter Weather Analysis, Upper-Air & Thermodynamic Analysis, Probabilistic / Ensemble Forecast Brief
- **Competencies:** winter-weather, ptype, banding

### Year II Week 23 · Program Week 59 — Boundary Layer, Fire Weather & Complex Terrain
- **Monday — Dynamics / Theory:** Study turbulent mixing, stability, diurnal boundary-layer evolution, downslope/upslope flow, gap winds and fire-weather ingredients.
- **Tuesday — Quantitative Methods:** Apply flux/gradient concepts, Richardson-number intuition and terrain-adjusted wind reasoning.
- **Wednesday — Analysis Laboratory:** Analyze surface/upper-air data for mixing height, humidity recovery, wind shifts and terrain effects.
- **Thursday — Remote Sensing / NWP:** Use high-resolution guidance, satellite fire/smoke context and observations while respecting hotspot limitations.
- **Friday — Operational Forecast Shift:** Produce a fire-weather/terrain briefing focused on wind, humidity, stability and timing.
- **Required forms:** Fire Weather & Boundary-Layer Forecast, Upper-Air & Thermodynamic Analysis, Professional Forecast Briefing
- **Competencies:** boundary-layer, fire-weather, terrain

### Year II Week 24 · Program Week 60 — UNIT VII CHECKPOINT — High-Impact Midlatitude Weather
- **Monday — Dynamics / Theory:** Synthesize Weeks 19–23: convection, severe hazards, winter weather, boundary layer and terrain.
- **Tuesday — Quantitative Methods:** Cumulative thermodynamic, kinematic and probabilistic calculations.
- **Wednesday — Analysis Laboratory:** Complete an unseen high-impact case and identify competing hazards and uncertainties.
- **Thursday — Remote Sensing / NWP:** Integrate radar, satellite, observations and model/ensemble guidance.
- **Friday — Operational Forecast Shift:** Deliver a multi-hazard decision-support briefing. Unit checkpoint ≥80% required.
- **Required forms:** Impact-Based Decision Support Brief, Operational Forecast Shift Log, Forecast Verification Scorecard
- **Competencies:** high-impact, multi-hazard, decision-support

### Year II Week 25 · Program Week 61 — Tropical Meteorology & Tropical Cyclone Genesis
- **Monday — Dynamics / Theory:** Study tropical thermodynamics, easterly waves, vorticity, moisture, shear, ocean coupling and genesis environments.
- **Tuesday — Quantitative Methods:** Apply angular momentum, vorticity and thermodynamic calculations to simplified tropical systems.
- **Wednesday — Analysis Laboratory:** Analyze a pre-genesis disturbance from satellite, surface/upper-air and environmental data.
- **Thursday — Remote Sensing / NWP:** Compare ensemble/genesis guidance and satellite organization trends.
- **Friday — Operational Forecast Shift:** Prepare a tropical disturbance outlook-style training brief without representing it as an official NHC product.
- **Required forms:** Tropical Cyclone Analysis, Multispectral Satellite Analysis, Probabilistic / Ensemble Forecast Brief
- **Competencies:** tropical-genesis, satellite, ensemble

### Year II Week 26 · Program Week 62 — Tropical Cyclone Structure, Track, Intensity & Hazards
- **Monday — Dynamics / Theory:** Study warm-core structure, eyewall/rainbands, steering, beta effects, shear, ocean heat, rapid intensity change and hazard asymmetry.
- **Tuesday — Quantitative Methods:** Use vector steering, pressure-wind reasoning and probabilistic track/intensity interpretation.
- **Wednesday — Analysis Laboratory:** Analyze an active/archive cyclone with reconnaissance/satellite/model information where available.
- **Thursday — Remote Sensing / NWP:** Compare official NHC products with model/ensemble guidance and identify why guidance is not the official forecast.
- **Friday — Operational Forecast Shift:** Deliver a partner-focused tropical hazard briefing covering wind, surge, rain and tornado risk.
- **Required forms:** Tropical Cyclone Analysis, Impact-Based Decision Support Brief, Model & Ensemble Comparison
- **Competencies:** tropical-cyclone, track-intensity, hazards

### Year II Week 27 · Program Week 63 — Aviation Meteorology & Terminal/En-Route Hazards
- **Monday — Dynamics / Theory:** Study ceilings/visibility, convection, turbulence, icing, wind shear, mountain waves, jet-stream hazards and aviation forecast products.
- **Tuesday — Quantitative Methods:** Apply density/pressure, wind components, freezing-level and probability calculations to flight-weather problems.
- **Wednesday — Analysis Laboratory:** Analyze METAR/TAF-style observations plus sounding/radar/satellite evidence for an aviation route/terminal.
- **Thursday — Remote Sensing / NWP:** Use Aviation Weather Center products with model and observational context.
- **Friday — Operational Forecast Shift:** Create a training aviation weather brief with timing, confidence and operational impacts.
- **Required forms:** Aviation Weather Brief, Upper-Air & Thermodynamic Analysis, Professional Forecast Briefing
- **Competencies:** aviation, icing-turbulence, terminal-weather

### Year II Week 28 · Program Week 64 — Marine Meteorology, Coastal Weather & Waves
- **Monday — Dynamics / Theory:** Study marine boundary layer, pressure/wind over water, stability, fog, coastal jets, convection and wave-generation concepts.
- **Tuesday — Quantitative Methods:** Calculate wind-wave relationships conceptually, fetch/duration effects and marine pressure/wind diagnostics.
- **Wednesday — Analysis Laboratory:** Analyze a coastal/offshore case including fronts, fog, gale potential and sea-state evolution.
- **Thursday — Remote Sensing / NWP:** Use Ocean Prediction Center, satellite, buoy/observation and model guidance.
- **Friday — Operational Forecast Shift:** Produce a marine forecast brief emphasizing wind, waves, visibility and hazardous transitions.
- **Required forms:** Marine Weather Forecast Worksheet, Professional Forecast Briefing, Forecast Verification Scorecard
- **Competencies:** marine, coastal, waves

### Year II Week 29 · Program Week 65 — Hydrometeorology, QPF & Flood Forecast Support
- **Monday — Dynamics / Theory:** Study precipitation efficiency, training convection, terrain enhancement, antecedent conditions, runoff response and flash-flood concepts.
- **Tuesday — Quantitative Methods:** Apply basin-average precipitation, recurrence/probability and simple runoff/threshold calculations.
- **Wednesday — Analysis Laboratory:** Analyze QPF, radar estimates, soil/river context and observed rainfall for a flood-risk case.
- **Thursday — Remote Sensing / NWP:** Use Weather Prediction Center and National Water Prediction Service products with radar/model guidance.
- **Friday — Operational Forecast Shift:** Create a flood decision-support briefing that separates meteorological forecast from hydrologic official products.
- **Required forms:** Hydrometeorology & Flood Support Worksheet, Impact-Based Decision Support Brief, Forecast Verification Scorecard
- **Competencies:** hydrometeorology, qpf, flood-support

### Year II Week 30 · Program Week 66 — UNIT VIII CHECKPOINT — Specialized Forecast Services
- **Monday — Dynamics / Theory:** Synthesize tropical, aviation, marine and hydrometeorological forecasting.
- **Tuesday — Quantitative Methods:** Cumulative vector/probability/diagnostic calculations across specialized services.
- **Wednesday — Analysis Laboratory:** Complete an unseen scenario requiring service-specific analysis and hazard prioritization.
- **Thursday — Remote Sensing / NWP:** Use the appropriate official center products and observational/model evidence.
- **Friday — Operational Forecast Shift:** Deliver two audience-specific briefings from the same meteorological evidence. Unit checkpoint ≥80% required.
- **Required forms:** Impact-Based Decision Support Brief, Operational Forecast Shift Log, Forecast Verification Scorecard
- **Competencies:** specialized-services, audience, hazards

### Year II Week 31 · Program Week 67 — NWP II: Model Physics, Bias & Post-Processing
- **Monday — Dynamics / Theory:** Examine microphysics, convection, boundary-layer and radiation schemes; identify systematic model biases and post-processing needs.
- **Tuesday — Quantitative Methods:** Use error statistics, regression/calibration concepts and bias correction on a small forecast dataset.
- **Wednesday — Analysis Laboratory:** Compare multiple model forecasts against observations and quantify recurring error modes.
- **Thursday — Remote Sensing / NWP:** Inspect deterministic and ensemble guidance for physics-dependent differences.
- **Friday — Operational Forecast Shift:** Write a model-bias memo and adjust a forecast only when supported by evidence.
- **Required forms:** NWP Diagnostic Worksheet, Model & Ensemble Comparison, Forecast Verification Scorecard
- **Competencies:** model-physics, bias, post-processing

### Year II Week 32 · Program Week 68 — Advanced Ensembles, Data Assimilation & Predictability
- **Monday — Dynamics / Theory:** Study ensemble perturbations, data-assimilation cycles, spread-error relationships, sensitivity and predictability limits.
- **Tuesday — Quantitative Methods:** Calculate Brier-score style probability error, reliability concepts and ensemble summary statistics.
- **Wednesday — Analysis Laboratory:** Evaluate an ensemble forecast for spread, clustering, outliers and observation consistency.
- **Thursday — Remote Sensing / NWP:** Use ensemble probabilities/plumes/spaghetti-style fields and compare with deterministic runs.
- **Friday — Operational Forecast Shift:** Issue a probabilistic decision-support forecast with threshold-based scenarios.
- **Required forms:** Probabilistic / Ensemble Forecast Brief, Model & Ensemble Comparison, Forecast Verification Scorecard
- **Competencies:** ensembles, predictability, probabilistic-forecast

### Year II Week 33 · Program Week 69 — Impact-Based Forecasting & Decision Support Services
- **Monday — Dynamics / Theory:** Translate meteorological hazards into impact-relevant information while preserving uncertainty and role boundaries.
- **Tuesday — Quantitative Methods:** Use decision thresholds, expected-value concepts and contingency tables to evaluate forecast usefulness.
- **Wednesday — Analysis Laboratory:** Build an impact matrix for multiple partners with different vulnerabilities and lead-time needs.
- **Thursday — Remote Sensing / NWP:** Integrate official products, observations, forecast guidance and partner context without altering authoritative warnings.
- **Friday — Operational Forecast Shift:** Conduct a simulated partner briefing, questions-and-answers session and update cycle.
- **Required forms:** Impact-Based Decision Support Brief, Operational Forecast Shift Log
- **Competencies:** decision-support, impacts, partner-communication

### Year II Week 34 · Program Week 70 — Forecast Verification, Calibration & Continuous Improvement
- **Monday — Dynamics / Theory:** Study deterministic and probabilistic verification, bias, MAE/RMSE, contingency tables, reliability, resolution and skill.
- **Tuesday — Quantitative Methods:** Calculate verification metrics and interpret when a metric can mislead.
- **Wednesday — Analysis Laboratory:** Verify a multi-day forecast set by variable/hazard and diagnose systematic failure modes.
- **Thursday — Remote Sensing / NWP:** Compare model and human forecast performance using consistent verification periods.
- **Friday — Operational Forecast Shift:** Write an after-action forecast review with specific corrective learning objectives.
- **Required forms:** Forecast Verification Scorecard, Model & Ensemble Comparison
- **Competencies:** verification, calibration, continuous-improvement

### Year II Week 35 · Program Week 71 — Operational Forecast Office Practicum
- **Monday — Dynamics / Theory:** Integrate the entire two-year curriculum under a simulated forecast-office workflow and shift handoff.
- **Tuesday — Quantitative Methods:** Complete rapid quantitative diagnostics selected from dynamics, thermodynamics, probability and verification.
- **Wednesday — Analysis Laboratory:** Perform surface/upper-air, radar, satellite, model and ensemble analysis under a fixed briefing deadline.
- **Thursday — Remote Sensing / NWP:** Maintain a source/valid-time log and resolve conflicting guidance through documented reasoning.
- **Friday — Operational Forecast Shift:** Complete a full forecast shift: analysis → forecast → discussion → partner brief → verification plan → handoff.
- **Required forms:** Operational Forecast Shift Log, Synoptic Diagnostic Worksheet, Professional Doppler Radar Analysis, Multispectral Satellite Analysis, Model & Ensemble Comparison, Impact-Based Decision Support Brief
- **Competencies:** operations, time-management, handoff

### Year II Week 36 · Program Week 72 — YEAR II CAPSTONE — Professional Meteorologist Qualification Review

- **Tuesday — Quantitative Methods:** Complete quantitative ODE/dynamics/thermodynamics/probability/verification problems without averaging away weak domains.
- **Wednesday — Analysis Laboratory:** Analyze an unseen multi-hazard case and document the full evidence chain.
- **Thursday — Remote Sensing / NWP:** Integrate radar, satellite, observations, deterministic/ensemble guidance and official center products.
- **Friday — Operational Forecast Shift:** Complete the final simulated operational shift, written forecast discussion, decision-support briefing, portfolio defense and oral examination. Every component ≥80%.
- **Required forms:** Capstone Operational Shift Packet, Forecast Verification Scorecard, Impact-Based Decision Support Brief
