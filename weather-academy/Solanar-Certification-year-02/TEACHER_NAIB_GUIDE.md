# Teacher & NAIB Mentor Guide — Year II

NAIB and human mentors should use the same prerequisite rule: identify the first unmet gate and remediate that competency before recommending advancement.

## Mentor priorities
- Ask the learner to explain the physical reasoning, not merely name a model product.
- Require source, valid time, observation/model distinction and uncertainty statements.
- Treat official warnings/advisories as authoritative products, not outputs to be recreated by a training simulation.
- Require forecast verification and after-action learning.
- Challenge model over-reliance and unsupported certainty.
- During capstone work, assess timeliness, synthesis, communication, handoff quality and willingness to update when evidence changes.

## Practical scoring
Recommended rubric: science/diagnosis 30%, quantitative reasoning 15%, multi-source synthesis 20%, forecast/decision quality 15%, uncertainty/source discipline 10%, communication/handoff 10%. Each critical competency must still meet the 80% mastery policy where separately assessed.
