# Mastery, Assessment & Credential Rules — Year II

## Non-compensatory 80% rule
Every required gate must independently reach 80%. Strong performance in one domain cannot average away a failed prerequisite.

### Weekly sequence
1. Monday daily exit ≥80%
2. Tuesday daily exit ≥80%
3. Wednesday daily exit ≥80%
4. Thursday daily exit ≥80%
5. Friday daily exit ≥80%
6. Required professional forms completed
7. Weekly 10-question mastery assessment ≥80%
8. Mentor/instructor-verified weekly practical or forecast-shift score ≥80%
9. Every sixth week: cumulative unit checkpoint ≥80%

### Semester sequence
All 18 weeks and all unit checkpoints must be complete before the semester comprehensive is recorded. The comprehensive written/practical components must reach ≥80%.

### Year II final
The final opens only after Semester IV completion. Four components each require ≥80%:
- 80-question cumulative written examination
- simulated operational forecast shift
- portfolio/professional forms defense
- oral meteorology examination / briefing defense

### Certificate wording
The unlocked credential is a Solanar/Khaemenes **Professional Meteorology Certificate of Completion — Meteorologist Track**. It is a certificate of completion, not a government license, AMS certification, university degree, or guarantee of employment eligibility.
