# High School Standards Alignment — Weather 301–304

The sequence is designed as a rigorous meteorology specialization compatible with major NGSS high-school Earth and environmental science expectations.

Core connections include:

- HS-ESS2-2 — Earth-system changes and feedbacks.
- HS-ESS2-4 — Models of energy flow into/out of Earth systems and climate.
- HS-ESS2-5 — Investigation of water properties and their Earth-system roles.
- HS-ESS2-6 — Quantitative carbon-cycle modeling.
- HS-ESS3-1 — Evidence-based explanations connecting natural hazards, resources, climate and human activity.
- HS-ESS3-5 — Analysis of geoscience data and climate-model results for evidence-based forecasts.
- HS-ESS3-6 — Computational representations of modified Earth-system relationships.
- HS-ETS1 design practices — criteria, constraints, evaluation and modeling of solutions where resilience/engineering is used.

Weather 301–303 extend beyond the minimum Earth-science performance expectations into college-preparatory meteorology: thermodynamics, station/sounding analysis, radar, satellite, synoptic/mesoscale diagnosis, numerical prediction, verification and operational forecast communication.

Weather 304 integrates climate dynamics, computational meteorology, model evaluation, statistics, uncertainty and a senior research capstone.
