# Teacher / Mentor Guide — Weather 301–304

## Weekly rhythm

Monday — Theory, Phenomenon & Hypothesis  
Tuesday — Quantitative Laboratory  
Wednesday — Data & Model Analysis  
Thursday — Operational Analysis & Research  
Friday — Forecast Briefing, Defense & Mastery

## Hard gate

The system is intentionally non-skippable through its normal learner interface.

A learner must earn at least 80% on each daily exit check before the next day becomes available. After all five daily checks are mastered, the weekly quiz unlocks. Weekly mastery is required for the next week. At the end of every six-week unit, a unit checkpoint must be mastered before the next unit. The final unlocks only after every preceding gate is satisfied.

## NAIB

Use **Copy Mentor Brief** during mentorship. It reports the current unmet prerequisite. If any prerequisite is under 80%, remediate that prerequisite rather than routing the student forward.

## Scientific source discipline

Students must distinguish observations, official alerts, forecasts, numerical model guidance, derived analysis, research/background sources and news reporting. An educational model or derived Solanar visualization never becomes an official warning.
