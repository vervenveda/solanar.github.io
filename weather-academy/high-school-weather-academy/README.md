# Solanar High School Weather Academy

## Weather 301–304

- Weather 301 — Grade 9 recommended — Atmospheric Science I
- Weather 302 — Grade 10 recommended — Synoptic & Mesoscale Meteorology
- Weather 303 — Grade 11 recommended — Operational Forecasting & Remote Sensing
- Weather 304 — Grade 12 recommended — Climate Dynamics & Computational Meteorology

Each course contains 36 weeks × 5 days = 180 laboratory learning days.

## Hard mastery architecture

Every required assessment is gate-kept.

- Daily exit check: 5 questions; 4/5 = 80%
- A failed daily exit check blocks the next day.
- Weekly quiz: 10 questions; 8/10 = 80%
- Weekly quiz remains locked until all five daily exits are mastered.
- Every 6 weeks: unit checkpoint; 80% required.
- The next unit remains locked until the checkpoint is mastered.
- Final: 60 questions; 80% required.
- Final remains locked until 180 daily exits, 36 weekly quizzes and 6 unit checkpoints are mastered.
- Certificate appears only after final mastery.

Courses are recommended by grade but may be assigned by NAIB/mentor based on readiness.

No private API keys, passwords, tokens, payment secrets or private backend configuration are included.
