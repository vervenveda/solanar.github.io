Weather Academy

A progressive weather and meteorology curriculum inside Solanar. Courses are recommended by grade but open by readiness, allowing NAIB, mentors, parents, and educators to route each learner to the right starting point.

101–105

Elementary Weather

Grades 1–5 recommended. Five complete 36-week courses progressing from observation and measurement to forecasting, radar, atmosphere, and introductory climate systems.

201–203

Middle Weather

Grades 6–8 recommended. Three complete 36-week courses in atmospheric physics, dynamic weather, hazards, forecasting, global circulation, climate evidence, and Earth-systems modeling.

Mentor-directed progression

The numbering indicates increasing depth rather than a rigid age gate. A learner can review a lower level for foundation repair or move forward when demonstrated mastery supports acceleration. Weekly and final mastery remain set at 80%.
