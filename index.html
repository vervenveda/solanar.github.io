Khaemenes Academy · Solanar Weather Academy

Weather 101 through 304

A 12-grade atmospheric-science ladder. Grade labels are recommended starting points; readiness and mastery determine advancement. Required gated work must reach 80% before later work unlocks.

101–105

Elementary Weather

Grades 1–5 recommended. Observation, instruments, water cycle, clouds, hazards, forecasting, radar, climate and increasingly scientific weather records.

Open 101–105Weather Portal
201–203

Middle Weather

Grades 6–8 recommended. Atmospheric foundations, dynamic weather, severe hazards, global circulation, evidence, modeling, forecasting and Earth systems.

Open 201–203
301–304

High School Weather

Grades 9–12 recommended. Thermodynamics, synoptic and mesoscale meteorology, radar, satellite, NWP, operations, climate dynamics and computational meteorology.

Open 301–304TraceLab

Professional Meteorology Certificate

Two years from foundation to operations.

A four-semester professional sequence designed around atmospheric science, university mathematics and physics, scientific programming, observation, radar, satellite, models, forecast communication and professional evidence.

Year I · Semesters I & II

Atmospheric Science Common Core

Foundations of Atmospheric Science, Calculus I–II, University Physics I–II, Scientific Programming, Weather Data, Thermodynamics, Cloud Physics, Observations, Synoptic Analysis, Remote Sensing, Statistics and Communication.
