# solanar.github.io

At its heart, Solanar Planetarium™ is a fusion of:

​

🌍 Earth Weather
Live readings displayed with clarity and precision.
A simple search brings instant atmospheric insight.

​

🌞 Space Weather
Links to solar observatories, solar storms, magnetic field readings, and heliophysics tools.

🛰️ Satellites & Planetary Systems
Real-time tracking, 3D explorers, mission data, and cosmic perspectives.

​

👧 STEM Education
Trusted resources, learning portals, and kid-friendly astronomy sites.
A perfect companion for Khaemenes Academy’s science curriculum.​
