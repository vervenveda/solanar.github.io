# Solanar Observatory

**Weather · Earth · Sky · Learning**

Solanar Observatory is a free, public weather and Earth-science gateway created by **Jennifer Kay Pearl · Verve N Veda · Khaemenes Academy**.

The landing page is designed to make weather information easy to find while adapting to the person using it. Students receive a learning-centered experience connected to Kids SkyLab, grade-level exploration, research tools, mentor support, and Khaemenes Academy portals. Parents and adult visitors receive a streamlined weather dashboard with current conditions, radar, forecasts, alerts, environmental news, and access to Solanar's advanced observation tools.

---

## The Solanar Experience

Solanar brings practical weather information and Earth-science learning together in one welcoming interface.

Visitors can:

- Check current weather
- Search worldwide locations
- Use device location when permitted
- View a 7-day forecast
- Open radar quickly
- Review active U.S. National Weather Service alerts when available
- Read world weather and environmental news
- Explore Moon and night-sky tools
- Open advanced Solanar weather applications
- Access student research and mentor tools
- Move directly into Khaemenes Academy school portals
- Switch between Student and Parent / Adult views

The goal is simple: **make weather useful, understandable, and easy to explore.**

Selecting **Student** changes the landing page into a learning-focused weather gateway.

- **K–2 · Sky Sprouts**

- **Grades 6–8 · Storm Lab**
- **Grades 9–12 · Atmosphere Lab**

The selected level changes the type of guidance and research expectations presented to the learner.

A research and discovery gateway students can use to investigate weather, climate, oceans, Earth science, environmental events, and related questions.

### N · NAIB Mentor
A connected mentor experience for asking questions, examining evidence, organizing research, and developing deeper understanding.

Age-aware weather and environmental reporting for learners from early grades through high school.

### 🎓 Khaemenes Academy Portals
Direct pathways to the learner's current educational campus.

## App Windows

Apps opened from the Solanar landing page are presented in large on-page application windows whenever possible.

Each window includes:

- A clear **× Close** control
- Escape-key closing
- Background-click closing
- An **Open in New Tab** option

This keeps the Solanar landing page available as the visitor's home base while they explore connected tools.

Some external websites may choose not to allow embedded viewing. In those cases, the visitor can use the new-tab option.

## Research & Mentoring

Solanar encourages learners to move beyond simply reading a forecast.

Students are encouraged to ask:

- What is happening?
- What measurements support that conclusion?
- Where did the information come from?
- Is this an observation, forecast, warning, or interpretation?
- How confident is the source?
- What other evidence should be checked?

PLERA Search and NAIB provide connected pathways for continuing that research.

## Safety

Weather can become dangerous quickly.

When severe weather is occurring:

1. Follow official emergency instructions.
2. Use official warning products for safety decisions.
3. Do not rely on educational visualizations as emergency guidance.
4. Children should review serious weather alerts with a trusted adult.

Solanar is an educational and informational gateway and does not replace emergency-management agencies or official meteorological services.
